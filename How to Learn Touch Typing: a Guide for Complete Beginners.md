## What Is Touch Typing?
Touch typing is an ability to type fluidly on muscle memory rather than visual searching to locate keys. Here is what defines it:
- Driven by Muscle Memory
- All ten fingers rest on a designated set of Home Row.
- Your eyes remain locked on your monitor at all times.
- Your mental energy is focused on writing.

## Step 2: Focus on Muscle Memory
Building a muscle memory is all about repetitive consistent practice. It isn’t magic. But the result of thousands of correct repetition.

> "I fear not the man who has practiced 10,000 kicks once, but I fear the man who has practiced one kick 10,000 times” ~ Brue Lee 

Practicing frequent, short sessions beat occasionall long session. Consistency always beat intensity. 20 minutes of focused daily practice is more effective than 2 hours on Sunday. The key is consistency.

The goal is to keep reinforcing the correct movements until they became automatic.

Your speed and accuracy will gradually increase as you practice regularly.

## Step 3: Building the habit
Some day you don’t feel like practicing, some day you are tired, or busy. Skipping the practice  sessions seems normal at the beginning. Motivation is unreliable.

So, Instead of waiting for motivation to practice. I picked fixed time. I didn’t have to have exact time, I could do daily just 20 min session any time I’m available.

Anyone can find fifteen minutes.

A single one-hour practice session won't change much. But twenty minutes every day for a month will. The goal shouldn’t be to become a faster typist tomorrow. The goal should be someone who practices every day. Speed becomes an inevitable side effect.

### Daily Practice Routine (10–20 Minutes)
1. **Warm Up (2–3 Minutes)**
Type easy combinations on Home Row (ASDF GH JKL: ), Top Row (QWER TY UIOP[]) and Bottom Row (ZXCV BN M,./). This is for reawakning your muscle memory before you begin your next touch type session.
2. **Accuracy Practice (8–10 Minutes)**
This is the most  important part of the session. Type slowly with correct finger. If you notice using wrong finger or looking at the keyboard, slow down immediately.
Don’t care about the typing speed during this phase. Accuracy is what matters.
Do Intermediate + Advanced Lessons from nativetye touch typing.
3. **Speed Test (2-3 min)**
Take a short typing test at Nativetype’s English typing test. You can force speed at this point. The goal is to discover and review result carefully. Pay attention to any finger that felt uncomfortable and which keys caused most mistakes.
This test tells what to practice next, not how good or bad your typing is.
4. **Retrain Weak Keys (5 minutes)**
If typing test shows that you repeatedly miss B, X or P, practice them immediately.
